(04) — The Ritual

Four steps to a better round

Step 01

Dress loud

Confidence is 40% of the swing. The other 60% is also confidence. Pick the shirt your playing partners will remember.

Step 02

Warm up weird

Ten practice swings, one interpretive dance, a single deep breath. Loose body, loose mind, low score.

Step 03

Play fast

Ready golf. Pick it up after double bogey. Nobody wants a five-hour round — least of all the group behind.

Step 04

Nineteenth hole

The only hole that truly matters. Settle bets, rewrite history, and order another round of fries.
